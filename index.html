Your mission
Precision & Safety from Stand to Dispatch

The Role
Your mission in this course is to become the ultimate guardian of safety and quality for Caljan conveyors. You are not just moving equipment — you are the final line of defense before our product reaches the customer.

The Action
By mastering this training, you will take full ownership of the lifting and packing process: from the very first electrical disconnection to the final hazard marking. Your goal is to execute every Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) with zero skips and zero errors, ensuring that required PPE is your second skin and non-negotiable safety rules guide your every move.

The Impact
When you complete all three modules, you will have the confidence and skill to independently, safely, and flawlessly prepare a finished conveyor for dispatch, directly reducing transport damage and helping your team move faster and safer every single day.
