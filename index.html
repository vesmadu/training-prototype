Assumptions

Every learning design rests on assumptions. Naming them makes the design honest — and easier to revise when reality differs.

ASSUMPTION 01

Learner identity

The scenario character is named "Alex" — a short form that works across English, Latvian, Danish and German. In reality, this work is performed by both women and men. The character does not define who the course is for.

What learners will achieve
Learning objectives

By the end of this course, learners will be able to:

🦺
Work safely at every stepIdentify required PPE for lifting and packing, follow all preparation steps in order, and apply non-negotiable safety rules — including the gravity sticker before removing the crane.
🏗️
Lift and pack a finished conveyor correctlyExecute the full lifting and packing procedures without skipping or reversing any step — from retract and disconnect through to film, labelling, stickers and hazard markings.
🔗
Understand how the two instructions connectExplain the handoff point where lifting ends and packing begins, and recognise that a worker assigned to pack must first confirm lifting was completed correctly.

✓ Expected learning outcome

After all three modules, a learner can independently and safely prepare a finished Caljan conveyor for dispatch — from production stand to fully packed and labelled — following all relevant SOPs.
